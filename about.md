**Small Worlds** is a collection of interactive simulations. Each one sets up a
few simple rules and lets you change them and watch what happens. Often the
behavior that emerges is richer than the rules suggest. I am making this for my own use and inspiration. But why not keep it opensource. I have used AI to do a lot of the coding. The goal is to simulate and learn using AI.

The simulations run entirely in your browser. Use the controls to change
parameters, pause, and reset, and explore at your own pace.

I'm Midhun Parakkal Unni, an Academic Fellow in AI for Health at the
University of Sheffield. My work sits where machine learning, dynamical
systems and signal processing meet, mostly applied to human physiology
and movement. Before Sheffield, I did a PhD in applied mathematics at the
University of Exeter, worked as a researcher at the University of Manchester,
and spent time in industry at TCS Innovation Labs.

Much of my work involves building models of systems that change over time,
and I've found that the best way to understand a model is to play with it.
This site is where I share some of those small worlds.
